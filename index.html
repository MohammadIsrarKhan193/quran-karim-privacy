Privacy Policy

Quran Karim by MÎK Platforms  •  Effective date: November 10, 2026

In short: Quran Karim has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Your reading progress, bookmarks and settings stay on your phone. The app uses your location only to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction, and only while you use those features. The Quran text and translations are built into the app, so you can read without internet.

1. Who we are

Quran Karim (the "App") is published by MÎK Platforms ("we", "us"). Contact: mikplatforms@gmail.com or via Facebook: MÎK Platforms on Facebook.

2. Information we do not collect

We do not ask you to create an account. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera data. We do not show advertising, we do not use analytics or crash-tracking tools, and we do not sell or share personal data for marketing.

3. Information the App uses

Location (optional). If you allow location access, the App uses your device location to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. Location is requested only while the App is open (foreground). We do not use background location and we do not keep a location history on any server. You can deny the permission and choose a city manually instead.

Data stored on your device. The following is stored locally on your device and is never sent to us:

  • your bookmarks, last-read position (surah, ayah and page), favourite surahs, prayer tracker, tasbeeh counts and saved duas;
  • your reading preferences (theme, font size, translation language, reciter, reading mode);
  • your last detected location (coordinates and city name) and about two weeks of prayer times, so prayer times and adhan alerts still work without internet or GPS;
  • your adhan settings (voice, length, and per-prayer adhan, vibration and reminder choices) and your daily-reminder setting;
  • recitation audio files you have listened to, saved on your phone so they can be replayed without internet.

This data is removed when you clear the App's data or uninstall the App.

Quran content built into the App. The Arabic Quran text, translations and adhan audio are packaged inside the App and work without internet.

Notifications and alarms. If you enable adhan notifications, the App schedules alarms and notifications on your device at prayer times, and may play the adhan audio through a short foreground service. If you use the daily reminder, a local notification is shown at 8:00 AM. All of these are created on your device and are not sent to us. They are restored after your phone restarts.

Sharing. When you share or copy an ayah, only the text you choose is passed to the app you select (for example WhatsApp) or copied to your clipboard. We do not receive it.
